# Gripper

Two-finger gripper: palm, pinion, carriages and jaws.

Print these in the order the files are numbered. Settings below are what the part is designed for, not a suggestion - the material choice in particular matters, see the note at the bottom.

1. **GripperPalm** - ASA, 0.20 mm layers, 5 perimeters, 30% infill, supports on overhangs only. Orientation: largest face on the bed, X axis vertical.
2. **GripperPalm (1)** - ASA, 0.20 mm layers, 3 perimeters, 30% infill, supports on overhangs only. Orientation: largest face on the bed, Y axis vertical. Not another palm - this is one of the two small stops that limit how far the jaws travel.
3. **GripperPalm (2)** - ASA, 0.20 mm layers, 3 perimeters, 30% infill, supports on overhangs only. Orientation: largest face on the bed, Y axis vertical. The second jaw travel stop.
4. **GripperPinion 16T m1p5** - PETG, 0.15 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 60% infill, no supports. Orientation: gear axis vertical so the teeth need no supports.
5. **JawCarriage A** - ASA, 0.20 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 60% infill, no supports. Orientation: largest face on the bed, X axis vertical.
6. **JawCarriage B** - ASA, 0.20 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 60% infill, no supports. Orientation: largest face on the bed, X axis vertical.
7. **JawTip A** - PETG, 0.20 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 30% infill, no supports. Orientation: largest face on the bed, Y axis vertical.
8. **JawTip B** - PETG, 0.20 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 30% infill, no supports. Orientation: largest face on the bed, Y axis vertical.
9. **GripperServiceCover** - PETG, 0.20 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 30% infill, no supports. Orientation: largest face on the bed, Z axis vertical.

ASA parts sit in the load path or next to a motor. Stepper bodies run hot enough that PLA creeps there under the permanent load every bolted joint carries, so those parts are not a place to save money. PETG parts are covers, gears, pulleys and brackets.
